# Excelerate-Data-Visualization

## 1. Introduction
Excelerate is an online global platform dedicated to empowering learners, institutions, and employers by offering transformative experiential opportunities. Through virtual training, scholarships, masterclasses, power skill courses, global internships, and career development programs, Excelerate fosters skill-building and bridges the gap between education and employment, enabling learners to thrive in an interconnected world.

As a team, we collaborated to create a dynamic and insightful dashboard for Excelerate. Each member played a key role in analyzing the data, designing visualizations, and ensuring the dashboard aligns with Excelerate's goals of empowering learners, institutions, and employers.

The purpose of this dashboard is to provide actionable insights into platform engagement, geographic reach, demographic patterns, and skill development. This collaborative effort ensures the dashboard is not only visually appealing but also strategically valuable.

## 2. [Dashboard Overview](https://lookerstudio.google.com/reporting/1a540d3a-7606-4f5e-88b3-a03a047dc782/page/p_0uali6g6md?s=mwbDLzca3PQ)
The dashboard is a result of meticulous planning and teamwork. It includes the following components:

1. **Platform Activity**: Highlights user engagement metrics (total profiles and opportunities created).
2. **Global Reach**: Heatmap showing user sign-ups by country.
3. **Opportunity Popularity**: Bar chart showcasing the most popular opportunities.
4. **Scholarship Impact**: Analysis of scholarship amounts awarded per opportunity.
5. **Skill Development**: Visualization of skill acquisition trends.
6. **Demographic Analysis**: Breakdown of gender, education level, and program participation.
7. **US City Insights**: Heatmap focusing on sign-ups from US cities.

## 3. Key Decisions and Design Choices
- **Collaborative Layout Design**: Prioritized key metrics at the top for quick access, followed by in-depth visuals for detailed insights.
- **Visualization Selection**:
  - Heatmaps for geographic data.
  - Bar charts for comparing opportunities and scholarships.
  - Pie charts for demographic data.
- **Interactivity**: Added filters for gender, country, opportunity category, and date ranges.
- **Color Coding**: Used Excelerate’s website colors to ensure visual coherence and highlight key data points.

---

## 4. Challenges Faced
1. **Data Quality Issues**:
   - Missing data in fields like Gender and Skills Earned.
   - Inconsistent date formats.
   - Spelling errors, especially in City, State, and Country fields.

2. **Performance Optimization**:
   - Large datasets caused delays in rendering interactive visuals.

3. **Visualization Clarity**:
   - Balancing simplicity and depth in heatmaps to avoid clutter.

---

## 5. Solutions Implemented
1. **Data Cleaning**:
   - Standardized date formats and filled missing demographic fields with "Not Specified."
   - Deduplicated records to improve accuracy.
   - Performed manual spelling corrections.

2. **Efficient Visualizations**:
   - Aggregated data to optimize performance.
   - Simplified heatmap designs for clarity.

3. **Collaborative Problem-Solving**:
   - Frequent team discussions ensured quick resolution of design and technical issues.

---

## 6. Insights Derived
1. **Platform Engagement**:
   - 1,000 profiles created, with an average of 3 opportunities per profile.

2. **Global Reach**:
   - Significant participation from North America, Asia, and Africa.

3. **Opportunity Trends**:
   - "Career Essentials: Getting Started" is the most popular program.

4. **Scholarship Impact**:
   - Over $21,600 in scholarships awarded.

5. **Skill Development**:
   - Most gained skills include communication, critical thinking, and technology literacy.

6. **Demographics**:
   - 59% of users are male, and most are undergraduate students.

---

## 7. User Interaction and Guidance
1. **Filters**: Enable users to explore data by demographics, regions, and time.
2. **Dynamic Visuals**: Allow drill-downs into specific data points with a click.
3. **Intuitive Design**: A clean layout guides users through the insights effortlessly.

### Visual Highlights
- Heatmaps for global and US city-level engagement.
- Bar charts for scholarship impact and opportunity popularity.
- Pie charts for demographic insights.
- Gauges for platform popularity.
- Cards to display skills earned, total skills awarded, and total reward amount.

## 8. Impact on Decision-Making
The dashboard equips Excelerate with actionable insights to:

- Tailor offerings to diverse geographic and demographic user bases.
- Showcase platform impact in skill development and financial support.
- Optimize marketing strategies based on regional and demographic trends.

## 9. Conclusion

4. **Strengthen Skill Development**:
   - Address skill gaps and collaborate with employers for market alignment.

5. **Maximize Scholarship Impact**:
   - Expand scholarship funds and align them with high-demand programs.

6. **Improve User Experience**:
   - Add interactive features, ensure mobile optimization, and gather user feedback.

7. **Foster Community Building**:
   - Introduce mentorship programs, discussion forums, and networking events.

8. **Future Expansion**:
   - Leverage AI for personalized recommendations and add real-time data updates.

By implementing these strategies, Excelerate can expand its reach, optimize engagement, and solidify its leadership in experiential learning and career development.
